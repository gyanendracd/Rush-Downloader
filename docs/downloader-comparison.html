RushDownload next to the downloaders people already use

Twelve tools, 31 features. RushDownload's column is what is built and tested in the project today, not what is planned. The other columns come from general knowledge of each tool as of mid-2026 and were not re-checked against today's releases.

Where RushDownload is ahead

  • One box for everything. Files, video pages, torrents and whole profiles go in the same link box. Only JDownloader and FDM come close, and neither covers all four.
  • Whole profiles, sorted. Photos and videos of a social profile land in Images and Videos folders. Only JDownloader offers this among the rest.
  • Sign in inside the app. No extension or cookie export needed for most sites.
  • Free, no ads, modern look. IDM costs money and uTorrent shows ads.

Where it is behind

  • No browser extension. IDM, FDM, XDM and AB Download Manager all catch downloads straight from the browser.
  • Windows only. FDM, JDownloader, Motrix and AB Download Manager also run on macOS and Linux.
  • No scheduler, no proxy, no seeding. Every desktop manager here has at least two of the three.
  • No playlists. A playlist link downloads one video. yt-dlp, Seal and JDownloader take the whole list.
  • No file-hoster accounts or captchas. That is JDownloader's home ground.

About RushDownload's marks. Video sites, resolutions, audio-only and streams work through yt-dlp, and profiles through gallery-dl; both must be installed. Profile downloads were tested live on one public site only. Torrents stop uploading when the download ends, by design. Sites that need a Google sign-in still need a cookies file.

About "Torrent". uTorrent and BitTorrent are the same program under two names, so they share a column.

Nobody here downloads DRM-protected streaming such as Netflix or Prime Video, and RushDownload will not either.
